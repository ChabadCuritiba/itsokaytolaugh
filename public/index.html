The quest

“I was on a quest to answer one question: What is true joy?”

It started as a question I couldn’t shake. Everyone around me wanted to be happy, and almost no one could say what happiness actually was. The more I learned, the clearer it became that joy isn’t a mood — it’s one of the deepest mysteries in creation, and our tradition has been taking it seriously for thousands of years.

This site is where I think out loud: essays, experiments, and a lot of laughing at myself along the way.
