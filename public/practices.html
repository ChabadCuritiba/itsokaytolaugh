Serious joy practices

Joy is a muscle.

Reading about joy is lovely. Practicing it is better. Three small tools — use them daily, laugh at yourself often. Your progress stays private, saved only in this browser.

Practice 01 · 60 seconds

1-minute laugh reset

Laughter changes your body before it changes your mind. Even a laugh you start on purpose tends to turn into a real one. Follow the sun.

  • Best done alone (or with someone brave).
  • Fake it until it isn’t fake.
  • Use it before a hard conversation, after bad news, or whenever you notice your shoulders near your ears.

Practice 02 · 7 days, then 21

The No-Complain experiment

Complaining is a habit wearing a disguise — it feels like honesty but works like a rehearsal of everything wrong. Try going a full week without it.

  • Tap Start. Every day without a complaint counts.
  • Slipped? Tap I complained. The streak resets — no guilt, just a fresh start.
  • Turned a complaint into a request or a thank-you? Log it. That’s the real win.

Practice 03 · 5 minutes

Joy journal prompt

Galut trains our eyes to scan for what’s missing. This retrains them to notice what’s here. One prompt, a few honest lines, every day.

  • Don’t like the prompt? Shuffle. No rules.
  • Write three lines minimum. Specific beats profound.
  • Entries stay on this device. Download them anytime.
